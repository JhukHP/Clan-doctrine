# STYLE_GUIDE.md

# Documentation Style Guide

## Goal

Documentation must improve practical performance.

Documentation exists to help players make better decisions.

It is not written for academic purposes.

---

# Tone

Preferred:

- professional;
- concise;
- neutral;
- practical.

Avoid:

- emotional language;
- bureaucracy;
- motivational speeches;
- unnecessary theory.

---

# Writing Rules

Every section should answer at least one question:

- Why?
- What?
- How?

---

Prefer:

Short paragraphs.

Lists.

Tables.

Examples.

Decision trees.

Checklists.

Avoid:

Walls of text.

Repeated information.

Abstract statements.

---

# Terminology

Use terminology consistently.

Approved terms include:

Caller

Squad

Squad Leader

Leader Partner

Master

Master Partner

Reserve

Rotation

SOP

Guide

Training

After Action Review

Do not invent synonyms.

---

# SOP Structure

Every SOP should contain:

Purpose

Scope

When to Use

Procedure

Communication

Common Mistakes

Success Criteria

Notes

---

# Guide Structure

Guides explain concepts.

Guides teach.

Guides are not procedures.

---

# Training Documents

Every training session should include:

Objective

Required Players

Preparation

Execution

Evaluation

Common Mistakes

Homework

---

# Examples

Whenever possible include examples.

Prefer realistic situations over theoretical explanations.

---

# Markdown Rules

Use headings consistently.

Avoid excessive nesting.

Prefer tables over long comparisons.

Keep formatting simple.

---

# Revision Policy

Improve existing documents before creating new ones.

Avoid duplication.

Link instead of copying.

---

## SOP Length Rule

SOP documents must be short enough that players can quickly remember the main actions.

If a procedure requires extensive explanation:

- keep the immediate actions in SOP;
- move explanations and theory to GUIDE;
- move practical exercises to TRAINING.

The purpose of SOP is execution, not education.

---

## GUIDE Rule

GUIDE explains principles, reasoning and decision-making.

GUIDE should not contain:

- direct combat commands;
- strict step-by-step operational procedures;
- long lists of immediate actions.

If the material answers:

"What should I do right now?"

it belongs to SOP.

If the material answers:

"Why do we do it this way?"

it belongs to GUIDE.

---

## AAR Rule

AAR is not a player evaluation document.

AAR must focus on:
- situations;
- decisions;
- processes;
- improvements.

Avoid:
- blaming individuals;
- emotional language;
- personal criticism.

The purpose of AAR is improving the system.
