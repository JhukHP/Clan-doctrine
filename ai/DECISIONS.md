# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

This document records architectural and organizational decisions.

Purpose:
- preserve reasoning behind decisions;
- avoid repeated discussions;
- explain trade-offs;
- provide historical context.

Rules:

- Every significant decision receives a unique ADR number.
- ADRs are never deleted.
- If a decision changes, create a new ADR that supersedes the previous one.
- Always explain WHY the decision was made.
- Avoid recording temporary operational decisions.

---

# ADR-001

Title:
Universal Reserve

Status:
Accepted

Context:

The clan has a relatively stable core but a constantly changing reserve.
Reserve players must be able to replace members of any squad with minimal preparation.

Decision:

Reserve is universal.

Reserve players are not permanently assigned to a single squad.

Consequences:

- Simplifies roster management.
- Improves flexibility.
- Requires standardized training.
- Requires unified SOP.

---

# ADR-002

Title:
Stable Squads

Status:
Accepted

Context:

Team coordination is more valuable than individual skill.

Decision:

Squads remain stable whenever possible.

Consequences:

- Better communication.
- Faster decision making.
- Higher trust.

---

# ADR-003

Title:
Permanent Pairs

Status:
Accepted

Decision:

Each squad is organized around two permanent pairs.

Consequences:

- Better mutual understanding.
- Easier tactical split.
- Faster recovery after regrouping.

---

# ADR-004

Title:
Caller Delegation

Status:
Accepted

Decision:

The Caller assigns objectives.

Squads determine tactical execution.

Consequences:

- Reduced communication overload.
- Faster reactions.
- Better initiative.

---

# ADR-005

Title:
Documentation First

Status:
Accepted

Decision:

Successful organizational practices should become documented standards.

Consequences:

Knowledge survives roster and leadership changes.

---

# ADR-006

Title:
Player-Focused SOP Format

Status:
Accepted

Context:

Players need procedures that can be quickly understood and applied during gameplay.
Long documents reduce adoption.

Decision:

SOP documents focus on immediate actions.
Detailed explanations, theory and training methods are stored separately.

Consequences:

- Higher usability.
- Easier onboarding.
- Clear separation between action and education.

---

# ADR-007

Title:
Training is Skill Development

Status:
Accepted

Context:

Regular gameplay does not guarantee improvement.
Players need structured exercises with clear objectives.

Decision:

Training sessions must have:
- defined objective;
- practical exercise;
- evaluation criteria;
- feedback.

Consequences:

- Training becomes measurable.
- Leaders can improve coaching skills.
- New players develop faster.

---

# ADR-008

Title:
Blame-Free Review Process

Status:
Accepted

Context:

Players avoid discussions when reviews become personal criticism.

Decision:

Post-match reviews analyze decisions and processes, not individuals.

Consequences:

- Players share information more openly.
- Problems are discovered earlier.
- Improvements become systematic.
